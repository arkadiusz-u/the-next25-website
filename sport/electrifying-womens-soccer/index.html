Electrifying Women's Soccer

When sport and electricity come together, they can inspire the world. Inside Hitachi Energy's partnership with FC Bayern Women.

FC Bayern Women × Hitachi EnergyFilm + Read5 minMunich · Feb 2026
Scroll to read

On 12 February 2026, at FC Bayern Women's stadium, two legends united around one goal: shaping a better society for the next generation. One builds championship teams. The other builds the grid that powers the world they'll grow up in.

On the pitch, energy is everything. The split-second sprint, the floodlights, the millions watching from home. None of it happens without power that simply shows up, every time. It's the same promise Hitachi Energy makes to the grid: reliability you never have to think about.

Why a football pitch?

Because infrastructure is invisible until it isn't. A stadium is a perfect, human-scale model of the system Hitachi Energy builds at continental scale: generation, transmission, and a moment of impact that depends on every link holding. The partnership with FC Bayern Women turns an abstract engineering story into something a stadium full of people can feel.

It also matters who tells it. The next generation of fans, players and engineers are the ones who will inherit the grid we build now. Resilience, high performance and clean power aren't just brand words here. They're what both a championship team and a modern energy system are built on.

From a moment to a movement

Munich was the announcement. The rest of 2026 is the proof: Tokyo, Sweden, Norway, New York. The FC Bayern partnership is the cultural spark that carries the message beyond the energy industry and into everyday life.

Industry · Topic

Everything around you was made in fire. That is ending.

The steel in the bridge, the cement under the street, the glass in the window - all forged at temperatures most people will never stand near.

The Next 25TopicThe electricity era · 2026
Scroll to read

Industry produces roughly a quarter of global carbon dioxide, and consumes about 37% of the world's energy, yet it is barely electrified. That is not because the technology is missing.

Heat pumps and electric boilers already handle industrial processes up to 500°C, covering food and beverage, textiles, pulp and paper and much of chemicals. What decides the pace is grid connections and tax policy, not invention. In the EU, taxes and levies average 26% of the industrial electricity price and reach 55%, while the fossil alternative goes untaxed.

By 2050 the furnace is a museum piece, and the factory is a grid customer.

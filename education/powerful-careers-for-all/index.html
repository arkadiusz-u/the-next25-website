Powerful careers for all

Closing the skills gap, one engineer at a time, across 60 countries.

By The Next 25Feature · 4 min readThe electricity era · 2026
Scroll to read

Balancing a demanding career with parenthood takes immense energy, in every sense of the word. Without reliable infrastructure, managing both worlds becomes an impossible daily battle. The grid is the silent partner of every working family.

We provide the reliable, resilient grid that quietly powers modern life, from the workplace to the smart home. And we are building the workforce to match: a company where women lead across the energy sector, engineers grow from intern to general manager, and 56,000 new people have joined the mission.

One career, twenty-one years

Fernanda joined as an intern before the company was called Hitachi Energy. Twenty-one years later she runs Transformer Components and Service in Brazil, with more than 200 people across field and factory. She has worked in quality, supply chain and plant management, and raised two children along the way. Her teams have broken plenty of paradigms, she says, showing everyone, most of them men, that each person is an important part of it all.

That is what a powerful career for all actually looks like: not a slogan, but a person who was given room to grow and now makes room for others. The grid behind her makes the daily logistics of that life possible.

Energizing the next generation

Powering the minds that will solve tomorrow's energy challenges.

By The Next 25Feature · 4 min readThe electricity era · 2026
Scroll to read

To solve tomorrow's energy challenges, education has to cross every boundary today. Yet traditional learning is still bottlenecked by infrastructure and distance: the classroom ends where reliable power does.

That is the quiet work of the grid. Energy is the warmth that welcomes a new baby and the pulse that keeps communities safe. It is also the connection that carries learning to students who would otherwise be unreachable. Three billion people already depend on Hitachi Energy technology every day, including the data centers and networks that bring education across borders.

Built by the people who will inherit it

More than 3,000 R&D experts in over 20 countries co-create the solutions the transition needs, working with universities and partners from Sweden to Indonesia. The next generation is not waiting to inherit the grid. They are already building it.

"I am proud to work at Hitachi Energy because we are contributing to a sustainable electrification of society. It is one of the most important things we need to do for humanity.Dilip Kota Elfving · Global Technology Manager, Västerås

Where the next engineers come from

Dilip leads the partnership between Hitachi Energy and Mälardalen University in Sweden: a strategic agreement covering research, skills development and talent supply, including joint projects on how AI and machine learning can help electrification. He describes it as a real win-win, cutting-edge research meeting deep knowledge of power systems, adding value for both, and in the long term for the whole world.

That is the pattern repeating from Sweden to Indonesia, where the collaboration with ITB drives innovation for the region. The next generation is not waiting to inherit this system. They are already in the lab.

One in four new cars is electric. Where does the power come from?

There is no transition without transmission, and every charge has to arrive from somewhere.

By The Next 25Feature · 4 min readThe electricity era · 2026
Scroll to read

Transportation is the backbone of commerce and connection, yet it remains one of the most stubborn sources of emissions. Decarbonizing how we move demands a colossal upgrade to electrical infrastructure.

We build the invisible nervous system of modern transit: end-to-end grid integration and hyper-scaled charging for marine, rail and land, all managed so the grid can flex but never break. It is the foundation that mass adoption of EVs, heavy logistics fleets and smart public transit will stand on.

Cities that move on electrons

Every electrified port, railway and charging corridor is a promise: that the way we move can stop costing the planet without slowing the world down. The technology is proven. The grid behind it is the build of the decade.

Coming soon to a planet near you.

A cure for cancer that arrives in your lifetime. Space travel that is not reserved for billionaires. A great school in every town on Earth. Sports gear so advanced it rewrites the record books, and every kid starring in their own story beside their favorite superhero. Children who are safer and more confident at the other end of every phone. Trains that outrun planes without costing the planet, and a climate that finally starts to heal.

The next 25 years could transform our world for the better, and every one of those futures comes with the same power bill. They need affordable, clean energy, delivered securely and at a scale the world has never built before. #TheNext25 is how we get there.

We are building it today for us, tomorrow for them.

Why 25 years?

Because a generation is the honest and essential unit of change.

Twenty-five years is the distance between the kid watching this film and the adult who will run the lab, fly the mission or captain the team. Every promise on this page can be measured on the way there, in new connections, cleaner power and cities that never go dark. That is real, visible, generational change.

So we did not say the future. We gave it a date.

Transformative
80million km
We have to rebuild the entire electric grid, in one generation.
Eighty million kilometres of grid added or replaced by 2040, which is the length of every power line on Earth today. IEA, Electricity Grids report, 2023
Clean
4superpowers
We are adding four superpowers' worth of clean power, by 2030.
The renewable capacity being built this decade equals the entire electricity capacity of China, the EU, India and the United States put together. IEA, Renewables 2024
Affordable
$38trillion
Waiting costs more than the whole American economy, every year.
That is the annual bill for climate damage by 2050. Paying for the grid is not the expensive choice. Not paying for it is. Nature and the Potsdam Institute, 2024
Secure
175+gigawatts
The wires that hold countries together are already ours.
More than 175 gigawatts of HVDC links integrated worldwide, carrying power between countries and across seas. The wire is the security. Hitachi Energy, 2026

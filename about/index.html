What is The Next 25?

Powering all our futures. How the next quarter century of electricity will redefine business, reshape culture and transform our world.

By The Next 25Feature · 4 min readThe electricity era · 2026
Scroll to read

Electricity demand is growing exponentially. The transmission supply chain can't keep pace, and geopolitical volatility is compounding the risk. Grid capacity has become the bottleneck of human ambition: our greatest plans are hitting an invisible ceiling.

By 2050, global electricity demand could reach more than 40,000 TWh, with emerging markets driving the surge. As the world's leading electrification company, we focus on the infrastructure where the constraint is most acute. By building grids that are reliable, resilient, secure, affordable and sustainable, we turn scarcity into abundance.

One mission, ten stories

The Next 25 unfolds as ten stories, from the stadium to the data center to the factory floor. Each follows the same thread: the clean power exists, and the grid decides who gets to use it. Start anywhere. They all connect.

Why you cannot just order a transformer

The bottleneck has a physical shape. Demand for grid technologies has outpaced global manufacturing capacity, stretching lead times for the transformers, high-voltage equipment and cables that every project needs. Behind that sit volatile markets for copper, aluminium and electrical steel, strained logistics, and a shortage of the skilled people who build and install it all.

None of it is unfixable. The paper behind this story sets out what closes the gap: long-term visibility so manufacturers can invest with confidence, faster permitting, standardization that lets factories scale, and workforce pipelines built years ahead of need. Scarcity is a choice we are still making.

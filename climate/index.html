The century's largest new electrical load is keeping people alive

Staying cool is becoming the biggest new demand on the grid, and it arrives exactly when the grid is most stressed.

The Next 25TopicThe electricity era · 2026
Scroll to read

In early 2025, wind and solar generated 30% of EU electricity, overtaking fossil power for the first time. Europe installed 19.1 GW of new wind that year, reaching 304 GW and heading for 439 GW by 2030.

But climate-driven hazards now outpace infrastructure built for a milder past. Our Spark Prevention Unit reduces the wildfire risk carried by electrical networks, as fires increasingly threaten not just forests but urban areas, agriculture and critical infrastructure.

Cooling is no longer a luxury. It is becoming essential to climate resilience, and the grid is what makes it affordable.
